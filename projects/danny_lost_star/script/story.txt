# Danny and the Little Lost Star

Danny was a friendly little green dinosaur.

He lived in a yellow house with a bright, sunny garden.

One evening, Danny went outside to water his flowers.

Splash, splash, splash!

Suddenly, he saw something twinkling under a big leaf.

“Hello?” Danny whispered.

A tiny star peeked out.

It glowed with a soft, silver light.

“Oh dear,” said the star. “I fell from the sky. Now I cannot get home.”

“Do not worry,” said Danny. “I will help you.”

Danny gently placed the star in his little red wagon.

First, he pulled the wagon to the top of a grassy hill.

“Can you reach the sky from here?” he asked.

The star stretched up high.

But the sky was still too far away.

Next, Danny stood on a tree stump.

He held the star above his head.

“Up you go!”

The star gave a tiny jump.

Boing!

But it landed softly in Danny’s arms.

“I need one more boost,” sighed the star.

Danny thought and thought.

Then he smiled.

“I know!”

He carried the star to the tallest sunflower in his garden.

The sunflower bent down low.

Danny placed the star on its golden petals.

“Ready?” asked Danny.

“Ready!” cried the star.

The sunflower slowly stood up.

Up, up, up!

At that moment, a gentle evening breeze swirled through the garden.

Whoosh!

It lifted the little star into the air.

The star floated higher and higher.

Soon, it was back in the dark blue sky.

“I made it!” the star called. “Thank you, Danny!”

Danny waved both arms.

“Good night, little star!”

From then on, the star shone above Danny’s garden every night.

It twinkled once.

It twinkled twice.

And Danny knew it was saying, “Hello, my friend.”

Danny smiled, climbed into bed, and fell asleep under its warm, gentle glow.
